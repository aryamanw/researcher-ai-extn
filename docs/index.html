Transformers dispensed with recurrence, relying on self-attention to relate every token to every other. Retrieval-augmented generation pairs a language model with a search index, grounding its answers in documents it can cite. Scaling laws suggest loss falls predictably with compute, data, and parameters, though the exponents remain debated. Sparse attention, linear attention, and state-space models each trade expressiveness for long-context efficiency. Evaluation is contested: benchmarks saturate, leak into training data, and reward pattern-matching over reasoning. Meta-analyses of reading comprehension find that annotation and spaced review outperform rereading. Citation graphs reveal clusters of related work that keyword search misses. A good literature review is less a list than a map: who answered whom, which results replicated, where the open questions are. Systematic reviews follow a protocol to reduce selection bias; narrative reviews trade rigor for synthesis. Preprints move faster than peer review, and the gap between them is where most surprises live.

A reading lamp for your research.

Research Companion reads the page you're on and surfaces related articles, papers, and reports — right in Chrome's side panel. Your own API keys, no backend in between, nothing competing for your attention.

Core loop

Open the panel. Get back to reading.

  1. 01 — READ

    Reads the page you're on

    A one-shot script reads the readable text of the active tab only — never tabs you haven't clicked on, never running in the background.

  2. 02 — SEARCH

    Searches with your provider

    That text goes to your chosen AI provider, which uses its own web search to find related pages. No separate search API key needed.

  3. 03 — RANK

    Ranks and explains

    Each result comes back with a short, honest note on why it's relevant — the product's actual trust signal, not a persuasive flourish.

  4. 04 — RESULT

    Click through, or don't

    Past sessions persist in History. Reopening one re-renders instantly — no new searches, no new cost.

Why attention replaced recurrence

The transformer dispenses with recurrence entirely, relying instead on self-attention to model dependencies across a whole sequence at once.

Later work paired these models with retrieval over an external corpus, improving factual accuracy on open-domain questions.

How to measure progress is still contested. Most comparisons now lean on long-context benchmarks, and they don't always agree.

No backend. No accounts. No telemetry.

Runs entirely in your browser. There's no server the developer operates, and no account to create.

Your keys, your connection. Page content and credentials go straight from your browser to the provider you configured — Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, or OpenRouter.

Local storage only. Settings, API keys, and your last 50 research sessions live in chrome.storage.local. Nothing syncs.

Active tab only. It reads the page you click the toolbar icon on — nothing else, and nothing in the background.

Works with the provider you already trust.

Anthropic OpenAI Gemini OpenRouter

Paste an API key directly, or click Connect to OpenRouter to sign in without pasting one. Each provider uses its own web search to find related pages, so result quality can vary slightly depending on which one you pick.
