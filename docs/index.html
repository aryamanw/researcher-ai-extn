Related research, inside Chrome

Find your next source. Keep your place.

Research Companion uses the page you’re reading to find related articles, papers, and reports in Chrome’s side panel.

Explore the example Download for Chrome

Open source. Bring your own AI provider.

A useful next step, right beside the page.

  1. Read the active page

    Click the extension while reading an article, report, or paper. Research Companion reads that page when you ask.

  2. Find related sources

    Your selected AI provider searches for related reading. Results appear beside the page with a short explanation of why each one matters.

  3. Keep the useful connections

    Open a source to continue reading. Your last 50 research sessions stay in local history, ready to revisit without repeating the search.

Your browser. Your provider. Your choice.

Only when you ask

The extension reads the active page after you invoke it. It does not monitor your browsing in the background.

A direct connection

Page content goes from your browser to your configured AI provider. There is no developer-operated backend in between.

History stays here

Settings, credentials, and research history are stored in your browser’s local extension storage.

Choose your provider

Connect OpenRouter or configure your own provider key. Provider accounts, charges, and data policies still apply.

Choose how you connect

Supported connections: Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, and OpenRouter.

Go deeper into what you’re reading.

Install the preview from GitHub. Research Companion is not currently listed in the Chrome Web Store.

  1. Download and unzip the latest release.
  2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome and enable Developer mode.
  3. Select Load unpacked, choose the unzipped folder, and pin the extension.

Open the extension’s options to connect OpenRouter or configure a supported provider key. Usage may incur charges from your provider.
